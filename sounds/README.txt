helicopter.mp3 plays every time the helicopter rotor is switched on with F.
Replace it to change the sound (.m4a/.wav/.ogg also work). If no file is here,
the browser's built-in voice says "Helicopter, helicopter!" instead.

oiia.mp3 loops while the OIIA cat spins (hold E). Without it the
game sings "u i i a i" with a small built-in vowel synth.

meow.mp3: the meow for every cat (each press picks one of the meows in the
file, pitched to suit the cat). tom.mp3: Tom's scream when he "meows".
